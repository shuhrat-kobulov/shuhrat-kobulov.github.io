Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

What this app is

"Pawnder Uploader" is a private automation tool used only by the owner of the Pawnder YouTube channel. It generates a daily chess puzzle video and uploads it to that one channel through the YouTube Data API. It has no other users and offers no sign-up.

Google user data we access

  • youtube.upload: used to upload videos to the channel owner's own YouTube channel.
  • youtube.readonly: used once, during setup, to confirm which channel was authorized.

The app does not read, collect, store or share any data about viewers, commenters or any other Google users.

How data is stored

The only Google credential is the channel owner's OAuth refresh token. It is stored as an encrypted secret in a private GitHub repository and is used only to upload videos. It is never sold, shared or transferred to third parties.

Limited Use

Pawnder Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Viewers of the channel

Watching Pawnder videos on YouTube is governed by YouTube's own privacy policy. Pawnder does not run any tracking of its own.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the Pawnder YouTube channel.
