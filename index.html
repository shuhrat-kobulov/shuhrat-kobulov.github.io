PAWNDER

One mate a day · Pause · Ponder · Solve

Pawnder is a YouTube channel that posts one chess puzzle every day as a Short: White or Black to move, mate in 1, 2 or 3. You get a few seconds to find the answer, then the full solution plays.

Puzzles come from the Lichess open puzzle database (CC0) and every solution is checked by a chess engine.
